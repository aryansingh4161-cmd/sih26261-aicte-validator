# Accessibility - Pramaan

What is in place (built) and what is not yet checked. No conformance claim is made: no accessibility audit has been done.

## In place
- **13 languages:** English and 12 Indian languages (Hindi, Bengali, Marathi, Telugu, Tamil, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Odia,
  Punjabi, Assamese, Urdu) from one switch on every page; Urdu pages are laid out right to left. Machine-translated offline;
  English is authoritative.
- **Plain words:** findings, letters and the institute to-do list are written in short plain sentences, each with the reason.
- **Not colour alone:** every status has a word next to its colour (green / amber / red / grey; lane names in words).
- **Text, not images:** tables and findings are real text (screen readers and zoom work on them); evidence images come with a
  caption naming the document and page.
- **Downloads:** letters export as text, HTML and .docx; the expiry calendar as .ics; case files as ZIP.

## Not yet checked (designed / to do)
- Screen-reader walk-through (NVDA / VoiceOver) of every page; keyboard-only use; contrast of the default theme.
- The page design is being redone separately (approved by Nishchal first); accessibility will be checked on that design.
